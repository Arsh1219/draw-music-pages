Privacy Policy for Draw Music

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Draw Music (shown on Google Play as Draw Music: Sketch Song Maker) is an Android app that turns drawings into music: you draw on a grid, and the app plays your lines as notes with built in instruments. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the app with package com.drawmusic.sketch.songmaker, published on Google Play by Billionweb.

The short version

  • Your drawings and songs stay on your phone. Every drawing you make is saved only inside the app on your device. Every sound is made on your device; nothing you draw is uploaded.
  • Exports go only where you send them. When you export a WAV or MIDI file, you choose to share it with another app or to save it to a place on your device. The app does not upload it anywhere.
  • No special permissions. The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, location or photos.
  • There is no account. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We use Google Firebase to count how the app is used and to receive crash reports, and Google AdMob to show ads in the free version. These receive device and usage information described below.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before ads are personalised or your data is used for advertising, and you can change that choice at any time.

Information that stays on your device

Your drawings, their names, tempo, key, scale, backing track and canvas color, and your app settings (language, last instrument, loop and note view) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Files you export are created on your device and handed to the app or folder you pick. None of this is stored on a server, sent to us or shared with anyone.

Advertising and your consent choices

The free version shows ads. Premium shows no ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised and whether your data may be used for advertising and measurement. Until you choose, ads are not requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app's Settings under Ad privacy. Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings under Privacy, Ads.

Subscriptions

Premium is a weekly subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We receive no card details, no billing address and no name. You manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services listed above, which act on our behalf or under their own terms as described. These providers may process information outside your country under their own safeguards. Your songs are shared only when you choose to share them yourself.

Retention

Your drawings and settings remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Files you saved to a folder stay there until you delete them. Google Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months. Crash reports are retained by Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Advertising data follows Google's retention practices.

Deleting your data

Delete a song in My Works. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything the app stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics data associated with your installation, or to exercise any other privacy right you have under local law, email arshpreet@billionweb.co with the subject "Draw Music data deletion". Because there is no account, we will explain what we can locate and what limited information, if any, we need. Never send passwords or identity documents.

Children

Draw Music is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use the protections these services provide.
