Common questions

How does a drawing become music?

The canvas is a grid of 32 steps and 20 notes. Left to right is time, and higher on the page is a higher note. When you press Play, a line sweeps across the canvas and plays every note your drawing touches. Flat lines hold a note, slopes play a melody, and every note snaps to the scale you pick, so it always sounds in tune.

What is the difference between Line mode and Pixel mode?

In Line mode you draw flowing strokes. In Pixel mode every touch fills grid cells, so you can make pixel art that plays. Switch with the mode button in the toolbar.

I hear no sound.

Turn up the media volume with your phone's volume buttons while the app is open, and check that silent mode or a connected Bluetooth device is not taking the sound. Make sure your drawing is not empty, then press Play.

Where are my songs?

Songs are saved automatically in My Works. Tap the three dots on a song to rename, duplicate or delete it.

How do I export a song?

Open the song and tap Export. Choose WAV for an audio file that plays anywhere, or MIDI to keep editing the notes in another music app. Then share it, or save it to a folder on your phone.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Google account you subscribed with, open Draw Songs, open Settings and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Draw Songs, Cancel subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged.

Can I change my ad consent choice?

Yes. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, open the app's Settings and tap Ad privacy.
